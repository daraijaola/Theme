Read Before Entering

House Rules

01

Everybody dances

Standing at the edge holding a drink is permitted for exactly one song. After that, the floor claims you.

02

Shine or go home

Sequins, satin, chrome, glitter — wear something that catches the ball's light and throws it back.

03

No phones on the floor

The best nights live in your memory, not your camera roll. The mirror ball does not photograph well anyway. It has to be felt.

04

The DJ is always right

You may request. You may plead. The booth answers to the groove alone.
